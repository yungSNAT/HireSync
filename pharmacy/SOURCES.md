# Sources

Every legal or industry fact built into HireSync, where it came from and how firmly it is established. All sources were read on 5 November 2026. Recheck any row before relying on it after that date.

- **Verified:** read at a primary source.
- **Secondary:** trade, law firm or standards body source, or a search summary of a primary page that could not be opened.
- **Needs advice:** the text is verified, but applying it here is a reading a lawyer should confirm.
- **Design:** a choice made for this software. No source requires it.

NSW legislation could not be opened directly, so those Acts were read through an extraction tool that returns selected text. Check section wording against [legislation.nsw.gov.au](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au) before relying on it.

## Likely to change

- NSW labour hire regulation, which has been promised but not legislated.
- Everything under the Medicines, Poisons and Therapeutic Goods Regulation 2026, which began on 5 November 2026.
- Privacy Act reform, including the rewrite of s 6D in the 2026 exposure draft.
- PD2019_003, which predates the Aged Care Act 2024.
- Aged care screening, with a national check being developed.
- The State Award, whose 2-year term runs from 1 July 2025.
